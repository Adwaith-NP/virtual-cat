🖱️ The menu

Right-click anywhere (or right-click the cat) to open the care menu. You can also use the tray / menu-bar icon or .

✋ Pick up the cat

Drag the cat — you'll carry it by the scruff like a mother cat. Drop it anywhere, even on another monitor. Shake it too much and it gets dizzy!

💗 Pet

Move your mouse back and forth over the cat to stroke it. It purrs. Click to boop — but not too often…

🍽️ Needs

Watch the thought bubbles: 🐟 hungry, 🌙 sleepy, 💩 needs a clean-up, 🧶 bored, ➕ sick. If it starves for too long, it will pass away.

🧹 Clean & fix

Choose Clean up → Scoop poop and click each poop. Fix the screen repairs claw marks and tidies files the cat dragged around. A scratching post saves your screen.

🐭 Play

Mouse chase turns your cursor into a mouse the cat hunts — hold still and it might catch you! Also try the laser, yarn (drag & throw it), a box, catnip and the brush.

🤫 Working?

Quiet time makes the cat nap in a corner without needs or mess. Hide cat removes it completely (bring it back from the tray icon).
