🖱️ The menu

Right-click anywhere (or right-click the cat) to open the care menu. You can also use the tray / menu-bar icon or .

✋ Pick up the cat

Drag the cat — you'll carry it by the scruff like a mother cat. Drop it anywhere, even on another monitor. Shake it too much and it gets dizzy!

💗 Pet

Move your mouse back and forth over the cat to stroke it. It purrs. Click to boop — but not too often…

🍽️ Needs

Watch the thought bubbles: 🐟 hungry, 🌙 sleepy, 💩 needs a clean-up, 🧶 bored, ➕ sick. If it starves for too long, it will pass away.

🧹 Clean & fix

Choose Clean up → Scoop poop and click each poop. Fix the screen repairs claw marks and tidies files the cat dragged around.

🐭 Play

Mouse chase turns your cursor into a mouse the cat hunts — hold still and it might catch you! Also try the laser, yarn, a box, catnip and the brush.

🖱️ The menu

Right-click anywhere (or on Iron Man) for the suit menu. Tray icon and work too.

🎯 Enemies

Deploy enemy drops a flying drone, ground walker, tank, drone swarm or a heavy mech exactly where you right-clicked. Iron Man engages automatically. Click an enemy to lock it as his priority target.

💥 Weapons

Repulsors (palm blasts), double hand blast for close range, homing micro-missiles from the shoulders, and the arc-reactor unibeam that cuts through a whole line. Attack here fires at the spot you right-clicked.

⚡ Power & armor

Flying and weapons drain the arc reactor; standing on the ground recharges it. Missiles reload slowly. If armor hits zero the suit goes offline, falls, and reboots.

🛫 Flight

He lives on the floor of your screens — to go anywhere higher he has to fly. Drag him and let go: his thrusters catch him.

⚔ Army attack & the Iron Legion

Pick a side and a size — up to a legendary 1000 attackers. Iron Man calls in up to 7 Iron Legion suits to fight with him; in epic battles a destroyed suit is replaced.

☢ Nuke

Siren, a falling bomb, then a mushroom cloud and a shockwave that wipes out every enemy on that screen. Iron Man runs for another screen — if the wave catches him, he goes flying.

⏰ Reminders

In the Reminders tab, set a time and what you need to do. At that time your buddy comes to the middle of your main screen, shows it and reads it aloud. Press Done, or snooze for 5 or 15 minutes.

🤫 Working?

Quiet time / stand-by keeps your buddy calm in a corner. Hide removes it completely (bring it back from the tray icon or by opening the app again).
